- [Shopify's broader maintenance guide](https://www.shopify.com/blog/website-maintenance): authoritative publisher competing for related broad intent.

The article adds a concrete record for each cadence, payment-test precautions, app dependency checks, theme-copy limitations and handover responsibilities. This describes our editorial approach; it does not assert that every competitor lacks these features.
