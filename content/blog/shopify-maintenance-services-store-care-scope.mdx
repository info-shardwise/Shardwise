Shopify maintenance services help merchants keep storefront changes, apps, integrations, and customer journeys under review as their store evolves. Useful coverage defines what the maintenance partner handles, what Shopify or another vendor handles, and who responds when an order journey stops working.

This guide helps UK and US merchants prepare a store-care brief and compare proposals. It focuses on responsibilities and acceptance evidence. Our [Shopify maintenance checklist](/blogs/shopify-maintenance-checklist/) covers recurring tasks, while [Shardwise's Shopify maintenance service](/services/shopify-store-maintenance/) describes relevant support capabilities.

## Map the store's operating dependencies

List the published theme, custom code, installed apps, sales channels, payment configuration, and connected fulfilment or inventory systems. Identify which system owns each important record and who has access to investigate a problem.

For a fictional homeware store, an app might manage product bundles while an external tool receives fulfilment instructions. A customer purchase depends on both behaving correctly. This example illustrates scope and is not a Shardwise client result.

Record current issues and distinguish initial remediation from ongoing care. A broken integration or undocumented customisation may need review before a provider can estimate routine coverage accurately. Ask for assumptions and missing access to be identified early.

## Separate platform operation from merchant responsibilities

Ask which tasks belong to Shopify's platform services and which remain with the merchant, theme developer, app vendor, or maintenance partner. Hosted infrastructure does not establish that your particular configuration and integrations are working correctly.

Define responsibility for product information, promotions, app settings, theme customisations, and connected systems. Clarify who approves changes and who communicates with outside vendors during incidents.

Do not apply a self-hosted WordPress care plan unchanged to Shopify. A useful proposal should describe the actual store configuration and the work the provider can perform within it.

## Review theme changes before publishing

Ask how theme versions and customisations are tracked. Identify important templates, app embeds, custom styles, and code changes. A provider should understand what the store relies on before recommending an update or replacement.

Shopify's [theme update guidance](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/themes/managing-themes/updating-themes) explains reviewing updated draft themes and checking custom changes and app compatibility. Use current guidance for the relevant theme. Do not assume every customisation will transfer successfully without review.

Agree the checks required before publication and the person who authorises it. Include mobile product pages, variants, cart behaviour, and relevant integrations. Record what changed and verify the live store afterward.

## Give each app a clear purpose and owner

Describe what each app contributes and which journeys depend on it. Review account access, recurring charges, configuration, and vendor support. An unfamiliar app should not be removed without understanding its role and stored information.

Ask how an app change is tested and how a failure is escalated to its vendor. Replacing an app may require migration, theme work, or integration changes beyond routine care. Agree those boundaries before assuming every replacement is included.

Record how app ownership and access are handled when staff or suppliers change. The merchant should understand which accounts and subscriptions keep the store operating.

## Check one order beyond the payment screen

Define representative customer journeys through product selection, cart, checkout, confirmation, and the systems that receive the order. Include important variants, discounts, delivery options, and markets where relevant to your store.

Shopify's [test order guidance](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/checkout-settings/test-orders) explains options for checking checkout and related configuration. Arrange testing carefully: customers cannot place live orders while payment providers are in test mode. Follow current instructions and verify the live configuration afterward.

Specify how test activity is separated from fulfilment, customer messages, and real transactions. Do not create unnecessary live charges or dispatch work merely to demonstrate a check. Agree who verifies downstream records and what evidence counts as success.

## Define integration failures in business terms

For inventory, fulfilment, customer management, or messaging integrations, specify what information moves and when. Identify which system owns the record and how failed transfers are detected.

For the homeware example, ask what happens if an order is accepted but the fulfilment tool is unavailable. Can staff see the failure? Is a retry safe? How does the team avoid sending the same order twice?

Record vendor contacts, access requirements, and investigation responsibilities. A store-care provider may coordinate with a third party without being able to change that third party's system. The support agreement should explain those limits.

## Plan promotions as controlled changes

Share campaign dates and identify what will change: collections, promotional text, discounts, landing pages, or app configuration. Agree a review window and the tests relevant to the campaign.

Avoid changing several unrelated systems immediately before a promotion without a clear reason and recovery plan. Keep a record of changes so the team can investigate if customer behaviour becomes unexpected.

For larger storefront work, our [Shopify theme development guide](/blogs/shopify-theme-development-services-guide/) and [shopping journey design guide](/blogs/shopify-website-design-shopping-journey/) provide related planning questions. A redesign or new feature may need separate delivery scope rather than routine maintenance treatment.

## Ask what can actually be recovered

Identify the theme code, settings, content, product information, and external records needed to resume operation after a mistake. Ask what is copied or exported, how it is stored, and how restoration is performed.

A previous theme can support recovery from certain storefront changes, but it is not a complete backup of every store record or app. Confirm coverage and gaps instead of accepting a generic recovery label.

Discuss changes made after the selected recovery point. Orders and external records may require careful reconciliation. Agree who approves recovery actions and what evidence shows the important journeys work afterward.

## Include performance investigation without promising scores

Theme code, images, app scripts, and third-party services can affect the storefront experience. Ask which performance checks are included and how findings become agreed work. Separate investigation from implementation when a fix requires changes outside routine coverage.

Measure representative pages and journeys rather than only the homepage. Review how new apps or campaign assets affect the experience. Our [Core Web Vitals checklist](/blogs/core-web-vitals-checklist-small-business/) offers related investigation guidance.

Do not equate routine maintenance with guaranteed performance scores, conversions, or search rankings. Ask for observable checks and specific changes that can be evaluated against your store's needs.

## Agree support terms and reporting

Define the reporting channel, support hours, issue priorities, and response expectations. Distinguish acknowledgement from resolution, especially when diagnosis depends on another vendor.

Ask how routine changes and incidents use any allowance and how additional work is approved. Only rely on actual offered terms; no universal package or monthly amount fits every Shopify store.

A useful report explains checks, changes, failures, unresolved issues, and actions needing merchant approval. Include dependency updates and known limitations. Your team should understand what needs attention without interpreting raw technical logs.

## Preserve ownership when providers change

Confirm merchant ownership of relevant accounts and appropriate access for staff and suppliers. Keep credentials in secure systems and agree how access is removed when responsibilities change.

Request an inventory, change history, operating instructions, and outstanding issue list at handover. Identify subscriptions and licences that depend on the provider. Another competent team should be able to understand the store without reconstructing every decision from old messages.

Review proposals using the same theme, apps, integrations, journeys, and campaign requirements. Compare exclusions alongside inclusions. The smallest quoted fee may describe different coverage from the one your store needs.

## Shopify store-care questions

### Does Shopify handle every maintenance task?

Platform services cover defined responsibilities, but your theme, apps, configuration, content, and connected journeys still need assigned owners. Confirm the boundaries for your store.

### Are theme updates always straightforward?

No. Review custom code, app compatibility, and important templates before publishing. Follow current guidance for the specific theme and test the resulting storefront.

### Can we test checkout without affecting customers?

Use an agreed test approach and follow current Shopify instructions. Payment test mode prevents live orders while enabled, so coordinate any use carefully and verify the live configuration afterward.

### Does a theme copy restore the whole store?

No. Identify which theme information, store records, and external app data can be recovered separately. Ask for the scope and limits of each recovery method.

### What should we share with a prospective provider?

Provide the store URL, theme, apps, integrations, important markets and journeys, known issues, and campaign dates. Arrange access securely and identify unclear responsibilities during onboarding.

## Define care around the order journey

Shopify maintenance services are most useful when they connect storefront changes with tested order journeys, integration ownership, recovery arrangements, and clear support. Ask for coverage that reflects your actual store.
