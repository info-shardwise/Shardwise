# The Evolution from SEO to GEO

Search is undergoing its biggest transformation in two decades. While traditional crawler rankings remain essential, answer engines and generative summaries now synthesize answers directly.

## The Pillars of Modern Optimization

1. **Semantic Knowledge Graph:** Exposing clear JSON-LD schemas (`Organization`, `Service`, `Article`).
2. **Topical Authority:** Comprehensive clusters answering precise, high-intent queries.
3. **Core Web Vitals:** Flawless load times and layout stability.

<Callout type="info">
  Generative engines rely on unambiguous, factual statements. Direct structured answers increase citation rates across AI answers.
</Callout>
